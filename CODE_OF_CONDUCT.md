# Code of conduct

Muru is a small project run by one maintainer. Interaction happens mostly in
issues and discussions, and this is how it is expected to go.

## Expectations

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.

- Be clear and specific. A reproducible bug report or a well stated
  requirement is the most helpful thing anyone can send.

- Be patient. The project is maintained in the time available, and not every
  request can be accepted or answered quickly.

- Accept decisions. Design choices are explained in the specification and the
  decision records. If you disagree, say why once, in the relevant issue.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing someone's
private information, and sustained disruption of discussions are not
acceptable in any project space.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, lock discussions, and block
people who do not follow this code. Concerns can be raised privately through
the contact address on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
