Each worker now times its garbage collections, by generation and by whether requests were in flight, and reports them with its CPU time and GIL waits: `muru_gc_collections_total`, `muru_gc_seconds_total`, `muru_gc_collected_objects_total` and the `muru_gc_pause_seconds` histogram in the metrics, a line per generation in `muructl debug memory` and the dashboard's worker view, a `gc` share in `muructl scale status`, and a diagnosis of "paused by garbage collection" when collection takes a tenth of the workers' time or more.
